Our mission

Roughly 88 million tonnes of food are wasted in the EU every year.

Most of it is wasted by consumers. But a significant share — millions of tonnes — never even reaches a household. It's destroyed at the manufacturer or the warehouse, because of labelling errors, over-production, slow-moving SKUs, or shrinking BBD windows. PriceMart exists to catch as much of that as we can, and route it to retailers who'll actually sell it.

Why this exists

Surplus food is a logistics problem dressed up as an environmental crisis.

If you've ever sat in a manufacturer's office and watched a perfectly good pallet of chocolate bars get marked for destruction, you understand the problem in five seconds. The product is fine. The packaging is fine. The brand is intact. The reason it's being thrown away is that the manufacturer's normal sales channels — supermarkets — have rejected it on a date threshold, and there's no internal team set up to find an alternative home for it before that threshold passes.

We are that team. For a network of brands across Europe.

"We don't make food. We catch food that's about to be thrown away and find it a home before it has to be."

How we measure impact

For every shipment we buy and redistribute, we capture three things:

  • Net tonnage diverted from waste — the weight of product that would otherwise have gone to incineration, anaerobic digestion, or landfill
  • Estimated CO₂e avoided — calculated using FAO / WRAP food-category emission factors
  • End destination — discount retail, regional grocery, social retail, or food bank

On request, we report these figures back to manufacturers so they can include diverted-from-waste tonnage in their ESG and CSR reporting. For retailers, the same figures support sustainability claims at shelf.

Where this fits in EU policy

The European Union's Farm to Fork Strategy sets a target of halving per-capita food waste at retail and consumer levels by 2030, and reducing food losses along production and supply chains. National regulations are tightening — France's Loi Garot, Spain's anti-waste legislation, similar pieces in Italy and the Nordics — all push manufacturers and retailers towards redistribution before destruction.

We sit squarely inside that policy direction. Working with PriceMart is one of the simpler ways for a manufacturer to comply, and for a retailer to source compliantly.

What we don't claim

We are a commercial business, not a charity. We make a margin on what we redistribute, and we have to — otherwise the logistics, warehousing, and risk of close-to-BBD trading don't add up. We are open about that. The reason the model works is that everyone in the chain — manufacturer, us, retailer, consumer — captures some of the value that would otherwise have been written off.

We do partner with food banks and social retailers on suitable stock, with preferential pricing. If you operate a food rescue network and want to talk to us about pipeline, please get in touch.
