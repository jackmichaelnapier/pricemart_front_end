About PriceMart

A Barcelona-based business with one job: keep good food out of landfill.

We're a registered European food operator headquartered in Barcelona, working with manufacturers across the EU and supplying retailers across Northern Europe. We're not a broker, not a listing platform, not a marketplace. We buy stock, take ownership, and redistribute. That distinction is the whole reason this works.

What we do

PriceMart buys overstock, close-to-best-before, and discontinued branded grocery from European manufacturers and sells it to retailers who'll move it through to consumers before BBD. The categories: confectionery, snacks, beverages, ambient grocery, pet food, seasonal lines.

Who we work with

On the supply side: manufacturers and brand owners with surplus stock — anyone from global confectionery groups to regional snack producers. On the buy side: discount retailers, regional supermarkets, wholesalers, food rescue networks, and online retailers.

Where we operate

Our HQ is in Barcelona. We collect stock from anywhere in the EU and UK, and deliver across Western, Northern, and Central Europe — Germany, France, the Netherlands, Belgium, Italy, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Poland, Austria, and beyond on request.

"Every pallet we buy is one not destroyed. Every retailer we supply is one not paying full list. Every consumer is one paying less for the same product."

Why we're different

The surplus food market has been dominated for decades by brokers and listing platforms — people who pass paperwork around without ever taking ownership of the stock. The problem with that model is that it adds weeks to every deal and exposes a manufacturer's surplus to the open market. By the time a buyer materialises, the BBD has shrunk and the value has evaporated.

We take ownership. When a manufacturer accepts our offer, we sign the contract and the stock is ours. We don't shop it around. We don't disclose its origin to our retail customers. We move it to our warehouse or directly to a retailer's dock, and we settle. End to end, typically inside two weeks of the first email.
