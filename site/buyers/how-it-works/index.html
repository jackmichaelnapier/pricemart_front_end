The process

Four steps, end to end.

Open an account

Send us the basics about your business via the form on the retailers page: company name, country, business type (discount, regional, food rescue, wholesaler), categories of interest, typical pallet volume, and the markets you sell into. We verify VAT and food-trade credentials and open your account — typically same day for established retailers.

Get the live stock list

Once your account is open, you receive our current stock list (PDF or Excel). It's also sent weekly to active accounts as new stock comes in. SKU-level detail: brand, pack size, case pack, pallet quantity, BBD per lot, wholesale price.

Place the order

Reply to the list or email your category rep with what you want. Pallet-out, half-pallet, mixed pallet — depends on volume. We confirm availability, send a pro-forma invoice if it's your first order, then a final invoice once payment terms are agreed.

Delivery

EU-wide delivery on our trucks or via partner carriers. Standard turnaround three to seven working days from order. Express options on time-sensitive BBD windows where the stock is moving fast. Delivery to your warehouse, distribution centre, or — for smaller retailers — direct to store.

Payment terms

Clear up front, scale up over time.

Standard terms for new accounts, with longer terms available once we've built a relationship.

First order

Prepayment by bank transfer (SEPA in EUR, SWIFT in other currencies). Once payment clears, we dispatch.

Established accounts

14 days net once you have a settled order history with us. 30 days on request for high-volume retailers with strong references.

Currencies

EUR by default. GBP, DKK, SEK, NOK, PLN on request. We invoice in the agreed currency; FX moves are not our problem or yours.

VAT

Intra-EU reverse charge where applicable. We need your valid EU VAT number to apply it. Our VAT ID is on every invoice.

Account requirements

What we need to open your account.

  • Company registration document (or equivalent in your country)
  • Valid VAT registration with EU VIES validation for intra-EU transactions
  • Food-trade credentials appropriate to your category and country (for example, a UK food business operator number for British buyers)
  • Bank account in the company name for invoice and payment matching
  • A primary contact for orders, deliveries, and account matters
  • A description of your business: retail format, store count, markets served, typical volume — so we can match stock to your needs

Most of this we collect through the account opening form. We'll come back to you if anything is missing.

Delivery

How stock gets from our warehouse to yours.

Where we deliver

Western, Northern, and Central Europe by default — DE, FR, NL, BE, IT, DK, SE, NO, FI, PL, AT, plus Iberia. Other destinations on request.

How fast

3–7 working days standard from order confirmation. Express options for tight BBD windows. We commit to specific delivery windows once the order is placed.

Volume options

Full truck, part-load, single pallet, mixed-category pallet. We'll consolidate orders for smaller retailers if you have a regular weekly slot.

Direct or DC

Delivery to your distribution centre, warehouse, or — for smaller buyers — direct to individual stores. We'll quote based on your routing.

The form takes two minutes. We come back within one business day with your account and the current stock list.
