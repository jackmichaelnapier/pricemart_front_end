Categories & brands

Branded grocery you can put on shelf without explaining.

Brand availability rotates with surplus supply — we can't guarantee continuous coverage of any specific SKU. But the categories below are always represented, with the brands listed below typical of what comes through.

Confectionery

Haribo, Ferrero (Kinder, Nutella, Rocher, Tic Tac), Mars (Snickers, Twix, M&M's), Mondelēz (Milka, Toblerone, Oreo, Cadbury), Nestlé (KitKat, Smarties, Aero, After Eight), Storck (Werther's, Toffifee, Riesen), Lindt, Lotus.

Snacks & biscuits

Mondelēz (Oreo, Belvita, LU), PepsiCo (Lay's, Doritos, Walkers), Pladis (McVitie's, Jacob's), Bahlsen, Mars biscuits, Kellogg's snack range, Lotus Biscoff.

Beverages

Coca-Cola Company range, PepsiCo (Pepsi, 7UP, Mountain Dew), Red Bull, Monster, Schweppes mixers, premium juice brands, sports/electrolyte drinks.

Ambient grocery

Kellogg's cereals, Nestlé cereals, breakfast bars, spreads (Nutella, Marmite-style), pasta, sauces (Heinz, Dolmio), condiments, instant meals, premium tea and coffee.

Pet food

Mars Petcare (Pedigree, Whiskas, Sheba, Royal Canin), Nestlé Purina (Felix, Friskies, Gourmet), treat brands, supplements.

Seasonal & novelty

Easter confectionery (eggs, bunnies), Christmas (advent calendars, selection boxes, novelty), Halloween, summer drinks lines, limited editions.

Listed brands are illustrative of typical inventory. Actual stock varies weekly. We'll match your wish list to current and incoming inventory.

Stock cadence

New inventory weekly. Same categories, rotating SKUs.

Surplus stock isn't predictable like a manufacturer's catalogue — that's the nature of the business. What we can promise is that the categories above are always represented, and the volume rolling through our warehouse is significant enough that there's always something live.

Weekly list

Active accounts receive our current stock list weekly. SKU-level: brand, pack size, case pack, pallet quantity, BBD per lot, wholesale price. PDF or Excel by email.

Wish list matching

Tell us what your shelves need. We hold the wish list and offer matching stock as it arrives, before the public list goes out. Particularly useful for retailers with consistent volume in specific categories.

Order minimums

Pallet-out for most categories. Mixed pallets possible for smaller retailers — we'll combine across categories. Full-container quotes available for high-volume buyers.

BBD transparency

Every SKU on every list carries the actual best-before date. You decide what fits your sell-through pace. Shorter BBD windows are priced more aggressively.

Pricing

Transparent, not negotiated.

List prices are on every weekly stock list — no "call for price" games. Prices reflect category, BBD window, volume, and current demand. Pricing is typically 30–60% off retail list, depending on the same factors.

Larger volumes and longer commitments get better pricing. If you're a multi-store chain with predictable category demand, talk to us about an annual framework.
