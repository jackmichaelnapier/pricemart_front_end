Who we sell to

If you sell branded grocery to consumers, you can probably buy from us.

Discount retail

Discount chains, hard discounters, value retailers, "pile-it-high" formats. Branded inventory at wholesale prices that fit your model — the same brands as the majors at a price that supports your value proposition. Most of our volume goes through this channel.

Regional & independents

Convenience, regional supermarkets, ethnic and specialist retailers. Smaller MOQs available. We can mix pallets across categories to suit your shelf.

Food rescue & social retail

Community shops, food banks, social supermarkets. We offer preferential terms on suitable stock — short BBD doesn't mean low value to the people you serve.

Wholesale & cash-and-carry

Multi-format wholesalers who supply restaurants, hotels, hospitality, and smaller retailers. Pallet-out, container-out, your call.

How it works

From enquiry to delivery in days.

Open an account

Tell us who you are, what you stock, and the markets you sell into. We verify and open a wholesale account — typically same day for established retailers.

Get the live list

You receive our current stock list — usually weekly. Pricing, MOQ, pack details, BBD all transparent. No "call for price" games.

Place the order

Order by email or via our reps. Pallet-out, half-pallet, or mixed depending on volume. Standard terms: prepayment for first order, 14 or 30 days thereafter.

Delivery

EU-wide delivery on our trucks or via partner carriers. Typically 3–7 working days from order. Express options on time-sensitive BBD windows.

Quality & compliance

Same brands, same compliance, just better prices.

Everything we sell is in original manufacturer packaging, fit for human consumption, with full traceability. Close-to-BBD product is sold transparently against the date. We are a registered European food operator subject to EU food-safety regulation. Full documentation supplied with every shipment.
