What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They cause no damage to your device. They are either stored temporarily for the duration of a session (session cookies) or permanently archived on your device (persistent cookies).

Session cookies are automatically deleted when you end your visit. Persistent cookies remain on your device until you actively delete them or your browser deletes them automatically. In some cases, third-party cookies may also be stored on your device.

Why we use cookies

Some cookies are technically essential — certain website functions would not work without them. Other cookies may be used to analyse usage patterns or display promotional content. Essential cookies are stored on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; we have a legitimate interest in providing the website without errors. Where consent is required (e.g. for non-essential cookies), processing takes place on the basis of Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and you may revoke consent at any time.

You can configure your browser to notify you whenever a cookie is being set, to accept cookies only in specific cases, to reject them in general, or to automatically delete them when the browser closes. If cookies are disabled, the functions of this website may be limited.

Cookies we set

At present this website does not set any non-essential or third-party cookies. The only cookies that may be set are strictly necessary for the operation of the contact form and basic site functionality. If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in the future, we will list them here and ask for your consent before they are set.

Server log files

Each time you access this website, our hosting provider creates protocol files that may contain:

  • the website from which you accessed our site
  • your IP address
  • date and time of access
  • the request from the user
  • the HTTP response code
  • the amount of data transmitted
  • the browser and operating system used

We do not use this data to identify you as an individual, and we do not consolidate it with other data sources.

Your consent

If we begin using non-essential cookies, a banner will appear on your first visit asking for your consent. If you continue to use the website by closing that banner or clicking "I Agree", you give consent to the use of cookies. A cookie will then be placed on your device and the banner will not appear again as long as that cookie is active. After expiration of the cookie's lifespan, or if you actively delete the cookie, the banner will reappear on your next visit.
