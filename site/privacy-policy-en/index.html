1. An overview of data protection

General information

This page gives you an overview of what happens with your personal data when you visit our website. "Personal data" means any data that can be used to identify you. For detailed information please consult the rest of this Privacy Policy.

Who is responsible for the data on this website?

The data on this website is processed by the operator of the website, whose contact information is set out in the "Information about the controller" section below.

How do we collect your data?

We collect data when you share it with us — for example, by completing our contact form. Our systems also automatically record technical information when you visit the website (e.g. browser, operating system, time of access).

What do we use your data for?

A portion of the data is collected to ensure the website is provided without errors. Other data may be used to analyse your usage patterns in an aggregated way.

What rights do you have regarding your data?

You have the right at any time to obtain information about the origin, recipients and purposes of your personal data, free of charge. You may also request that your data is corrected or erased. You can contact us at any time using the address shown in the "Information about the controller" section if you have questions about data protection. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

Under certain conditions, you also have the right to request the restriction of the processing of your personal data. For details see the section "Right to restriction of processing".

2. Hosting

External hosting

This website is hosted externally. Personal data collected on this website is stored on the host's servers. This may include IP addresses, contact requests, metadata and communications, contract information, contact information, names, web page access, and other data generated through a website. The host is used for the purpose of fulfilling the contract with our potential and existing customers (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and in our legitimate interest of secure, fast and efficient provision of our online services (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The host will only process your data to the extent necessary to fulfil its obligations and to follow our instructions.

Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

If processing is based on Art. 6(1)(e) or (f) GDPR, you have the right to object to the processing of your personal data on grounds arising from your particular situation. This also applies to any profiling based on these provisions. If you object, we will no longer process your personal data unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for processing that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or where processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

If your personal data is processed for direct marketing, you have the right to object at any time. This also applies to profiling associated with such marketing. After you object, your personal data will no longer be used for direct marketing.

Right to lodge a complaint

In the event of a GDPR violation, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in particular the authority in the EU member state where you live, work, or where the alleged violation took place. In Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), aepd.es. The right to complain is in addition to any other administrative or judicial remedies.

Right to data portability

You have the right to receive any data we automatically process on the basis of your consent, or in performance of a contract, in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format, and to transmit it to a third party. If you request the direct transfer of data to another controller, this will only happen where technically feasible.

Encryption

This website uses SSL/TLS encryption. You can recognise an encrypted connection by the "https://" prefix in the address bar and the lock icon in the browser. When SSL/TLS encryption is active, the data you transmit to us cannot be read by third parties.

Information, correction and erasure of data

Within the scope of the applicable statutory provisions, you have the right at any time to information about your archived personal data, their origin and recipients, and the purpose of processing. You may also have a right to have your data corrected or erased. For any such questions, contact us at the address above.

Right to restriction of processing

You have the right to demand restrictions on the processing of your personal data. To do so, contact us at the address above. The right to demand restriction applies in the following cases:

  • If you contest the accuracy of your data we have archived, we usually need time to verify this. During the verification, you have the right to demand restriction.
  • If processing was/is unlawful, you may demand restriction in lieu of erasure.
  • If we no longer need your personal data but you need it to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, you may demand restriction in lieu of erasure.
  • If you have raised an objection under Art. 21(1) GDPR, your rights and ours must be weighed against each other. Pending that determination, you have the right to demand restriction.

4. Data collection on this website

Cookies

Our website uses cookies. Cookies are small text files that cause no damage to your device. They are either stored temporarily for the duration of a session (session cookies) or permanently (persistent cookies). Session cookies are automatically deleted at the end of your visit. Persistent cookies remain on your device until you delete them or your browser does so automatically.

Some cookies are technically essential — certain website functions would not work without them. Other cookies may be used to analyse usage or display advertisements. Technically necessary cookies are stored on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Where consent is required (e.g. consent to non-essential cookies), processing takes place exclusively on the basis of Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; consent can be revoked at any time.

Server log files

Our hosting provider automatically collects and stores information in server log files that your browser communicates to us automatically. This information comprises:

  • type and version of browser used
  • operating system
  • referrer URL
  • hostname of the accessing computer
  • time of the server request
  • IP address

This data is not merged with other data sources. The data is processed on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; we have a legitimate interest in the technically error-free presentation and the optimisation of our website.

Contact form

If you submit enquiries to us via our contact form, the information you provide in the contact form, including the contact details you provide, will be stored by us to handle your enquiry and in the event of follow-up questions. We do not share this information without your consent.

The processing of this data is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your request is related to performance of a contract or to taking pre-contractual steps. In all other cases, processing is based on our legitimate interest in effectively handling enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) or on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) where this has been requested. The information you have entered into the contact form will remain with us until you ask us to erase it, revoke your consent, or until the purpose for which it was archived no longer exists. Mandatory statutory provisions — in particular retention periods — remain unaffected.

Requests by email, telephone or fax

If you contact us by email, telephone or fax, your request — including all resulting personal data (name, request) — will be stored and processed by us for the purpose of processing your request. We do not pass these data on without your consent.
