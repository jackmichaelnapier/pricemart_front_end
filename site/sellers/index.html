What we buy

If it's branded, ambient-stable, and edible, we're probably interested.

We've handled hundreds of categories over the years. The unifying thread: shelf-stable, factory-sealed, fit for human consumption, with at least 30 days of BBD on collection (sometimes less, depending on category).

By category

  • Confectionery — chocolate, sugar confectionery, gum, lozenges
  • Snacks — crisps, biscuits, savoury baked, nuts & trail mix
  • Beverages — soft drinks, juices, energy drinks, mixers
  • Ambient grocery — cereals, pasta, sauces, condiments, spreads
  • Pet food — dry, wet, treats
  • Health & sports nutrition — bars, powders, gels, supplements
  • Seasonal — Easter, Christmas, Halloween, summer lines

By condition

  • Close-to-best-before (any window — we'll quote against the date)
  • Discontinued SKUs from active manufacturers
  • Surplus production runs and over-orders
  • Labelling errors and rebranding withdrawals
  • Wrong-market stock (EU labelling on UK orders, etc.)
  • Damaged secondary packaging, intact primary
  • Customer cancellations and returns to vendor
Why us, not a broker

We're the buyer, not the middleman.

Brokers and listing platforms add a layer between you and the eventual buyer — and that layer takes weeks, hides margin, and exposes your stock to the open market. We don't list, we don't shop your stock around. We buy directly, sign the contract, and dispatch transport.

Speed

Most offers go out within 24 hours of receiving the spec. Pickup typically inside one week of acceptance. We move when BBD is the clock.

Discretion

NDA on request. We sell into retail channels that don't compete with yours — discount, regional, food rescue. Your brand integrity stays intact.

Logistics

Our trucks, our paperwork. Export documentation, palletisation, even repacking handled in-house. You ship from your dock and forget about it.

Sustainability

Every pallet we buy is one not destroyed. Your ESG reporting can include the diverted tonnage; we'll supply the figures.

How it works

Four steps from your warehouse to ours.

Send us the spec

EAN list, case quantity, pallets, BBD, pickup location, target price (optional). Email or use the form below. PDF, Excel, photo — whatever you have.

Firm offer in 24 hours

We evaluate against active retailer demand and reply with a binding offer. No "let me check around" delays. Yes, or a specific reason no.

Contract & collection

Sign the PO, we send our own transport. Paperwork, export docs, palletisation if needed — all handled on our side. Pickup usually within 5 working days.

Payment & reporting

Settlement on agreed terms (typically prepayment or 14 days against goods). On request we send back the diverted-from-waste tonnage for your ESG reporting.
