The mission

A tenth of all food in the EU is wasted before it ever leaves the supply chain.

Perfectly good branded grocery — millions of tonnes of it — gets destroyed every year because of labelling errors, surplus production, slow-moving SKUs, or short remaining shelf life. We exist to catch as much of it as we can, and route it to retailers who will actually sell it.

How it works

From your warehouse to retail in days.

Tell us what you have

Send us category, volume, best-before date, EAN list, and pickup location. Email or the form on this page. We respond within one business day.

We assess and offer

We evaluate the stock against our active retailer demand across Europe and send you a firm purchase offer. No tyre-kicking, no broker games.

We collect

You accept, we send transport. We handle paperwork, export documentation, and palletisation if needed. Pickup typically inside one week.

Stock moves, not waste

Your stock lands with retailers who sell it through before BBD. You free up warehouse space and avoid destruction fees. Everyone wins, including the climate.
